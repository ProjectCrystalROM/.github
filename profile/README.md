# 💎 Project Crystal

### We are the Crystal Gems. And we're here to save your phone.

*If you're an obsolete Android device, you can count on us taking a stand.*

Welcome to **Project Crystal**, where the spirit of *Steven Universe*, the stubbornness of Peridot, and an unreasonable amount of Linux kernel debugging come together to accomplish one thing:

**Make abandoned hardware useful again.**

Manufacturers call it end-of-life.

We call it a challenge.

## 🌟 Stronger Than You(r Bootloader)

Project Crystal is an independent Android development initiative focused on preserving, restoring, and extending the life of aging mobile hardware.

Our work includes:

- 💎 **Custom ROM development** — Bringing CyanogenMod, LineageOS, and other Android distributions to devices abandoned by their manufacturers.
- 🔧 **Kernel engineering** — Backporting fixes, resolving ancient driver problems, and convincing decade-old Qualcomm hardware to cooperate.
- 📡 **Hardware bringup** — Getting cameras, cellular radios, GPS, Wi-Fi, and other components working, one inexplicably missing XML file at a time.
- 🛡️ **Security backports** — Applying relevant fixes to older Android versions wherever feasible, without pretending that partial coverage equals modern security certification.
- 📚 **Source preservation** — Keeping device trees, patches, and development knowledge available for the next generation of technologically stubborn Gems.

## 💚 Peridot's Log: The Mission

The technology industry has a peculiar habit of declaring perfectly functional hardware obsolete simply because maintaining it is no longer profitable.

**UNACCEPTABLE.**

We believe older devices deserve more than a one-way trip to the recycling bin.

Not every phone needs the latest Android release. Sometimes the greatest achievement is making the version it *can* run stable, usable, and better maintained than anyone expected.

We don't measure success by how impressive a version number looks.

We measure it by whether the hardware actually works.

And if someone says a port is impossible?

Well.

**CLODS HAVE BEEN WRONG BEFORE.**

## 🌍 Earth Is Worth Protecting. So Is Its Hardware.

Electronic waste is a real problem, and extending the useful life of existing devices is one small way to push back.

Every functioning device preserved is another piece of technology that doesn't immediately need replacing.

We're not claiming to save the planet with a custom recovery and a kernel patch.

But even small efforts matter.

## ⚠️ Important Transmission From Homeworld

Project Crystal is an independent, community-driven effort inspired by *Steven Universe*. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cartoon Network, Warner Bros., or the show's creators.

Our software is experimental and provided on a best-effort basis. Security backports do not guarantee complete vulnerability coverage, and proprietary hardware components may remain unsupported or unpatched.

Back up your data, read the installation instructions, and understand the risks before flashing.

**We cannot guarantee your device will survive every experiment.**

We can, however, guarantee that someone was stubborn enough to try.
